You don't need a degree to start. You need a direction. Pick a track, follow the steps, and build real skills — the same way I did, starting from a gaming PC.

I  ·  How This Works
01
Pick a Track
Choose a topic that matches where you are right now. Never touched a PC? Start with hardware. Already comfortable? Jump to networking or home labs.
02
Watch + Read
Each track has companion videos on YouTube and written guides here. Watch first to understand the concept, then follow the written steps to build it yourself.
03
Build & Level Up
The goal isn't just to watch — it's to do. Get your hands on the hardware or software and complete the task. That's where the skill actually forms.
